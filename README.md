# Quantum Emotional AI

## Overview
Quantum Emotional AI is a novel framework that models human emotions as quantum states. 
Unlike traditional AI models that treat emotions as fixed numeric values, this approach 
represents emotions as superpositions with complex coefficients, collapsing into a 
dominant state upon contextual interaction.

## Key Features
- Complex-valued neural networks for emotional representation
- Collapse mechanism inspired by quantum measurement
- Potential applications in customer service, therapy, and social robotics

## Prototype
A PyTorch-based prototype demonstrates how text inputs can modify emotional states 
and produce probabilistic emotional outputs.

## Vision
This project introduces a new paradigm in AI research: combining quantum mathematics 
with emotional intelligence. It is designed to inspire further exploration and 
development in the field.
